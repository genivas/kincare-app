KinCare App

Official User Manual & Quick Start Guide

1. Welcome to KinCare! 💙

KinCare is your family's private hub for coordinating care, managing medications, and ensuring peace of mind for your loved ones. This guide will walk you through the essential features to get your family set up in minutes.

2. Getting Started

Creating the Administrator Account

  • Step 1: Access your exclusive portal at kincare-app.pages.dev/download to install the Android App or use the Web version.
  • Step 2: Open the app and click on "Sign Up".
  • Step 3: Fill in your Name, Password, and IMPORTANT: Use the exact same Email Address from your purchase. Our system will instantly recognize it and grant you VIP access!
  • Step 4: Once inside, you are the Family Administrator!

3. Customizing Profiles & Photos

Personalizing your app makes it friendlier for the whole family.

  • Navigate to the Settings menu (the gear icon on the bottom navigation bar).
  • Patient Photo: Click the small camera icon next to the patient's avatar to upload a photo from your phone's gallery.
  • Family Photos: Scroll down to the Family Members list. Click the small camera icon next to your own avatar or any member's avatar to update their photo.

4. Inviting Family Members (Free Access)

Your single subscription covers the entire family. You don't need to share your password or your account with them.

How to send an invite:

  1. Go to the Settings page.
  2. Under Family Members, click the + (Add User) button.
  3. Enter their Name and Relation (e.g., "Mary", "Sister").
  4. Click Send WhatsApp Invite.

This will open your WhatsApp with a pre-written message containing a magic link. When your family member clicks that link, they will be taken to the Download Gateway where they can install the app and create a free account to instantly join your family board!

5. Managing Medications & Routine

Keep everyone on the same page regarding what needs to be taken and when.

  • Adding a Med: Go to the Medications tab (pill icon) and click the + button at the top right. Fill in the name, dosage, and time. You can even take a photo of the medicine box!
  • Taking a Med: When someone gives a medication, they simply tap the Checkmark (✓) next to it. It will be marked as "Taken" and recorded in the History log with the name of the person who gave it.
  • Delete/Edit: If you made a mistake, just click the red trash icon next to a medication or task to delete it.

6. Offline Native Alarms

KinCare features an advanced, offline-capable alarm system.

  • When you add a medication, KinCare automatically schedules a native alarm on the device.
  • At the exact time of the medication, the phone will vibrate and show a high-priority notification: "It's time to give [Medication] to [Patient]".
  • Note: Ensure you grant the app permission to send notifications when prompted by your phone for the first time!
